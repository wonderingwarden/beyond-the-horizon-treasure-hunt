# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve Beyond the Horizon.

## Before submitting a change

1. Keep the site dependency-free unless a new dependency is clearly justified.
2. Preserve mobile usability, keyboard input, and the reduced-motion stylesheet.
3. Test the complete treasure hunt in a modern browser, including incorrect answers, repeated answers, the final answer, and map enlargement.
4. Keep map assets in `assets/`, or update their references in `index.html` if paths change.
5. Do not commit credentials, personal data, or material that you do not have permission to publish.

## Changes to clues or answers

The clue stages and accepted answers are configured in the `EDIT YOUR HUNT HERE` section near the bottom of `index.html`. Verify that the clue text, map highlighted, and expected answer match the physical exhibition before publishing.
